Paintwell 1.3

Faster
- Paintwell opens about twice as fast: its icons are now drawn once and kept, instead of
  being drawn again every time it starts.
- The snap is packed in a way that unpacks more quickly, so it opens faster, especially the
  first time after switching on your computer.

Tidier
- The code and its comments have been cleaned up. Nothing about how Paintwell works has changed.
