Paintwell 1.4

Live previews
- Effects and adjustments now update on the picture as you drag their sliders, instead of
  waiting until you let go.
- With very large photos, some effects need a moment to catch up. You'll see a quick,
  slightly softer draft while you drag, and the full-quality result follows a second or two
  after you stop. Picture frames, borders and drop shadows on very large photos update when
  you pause the slider. Pressing OK always applies the full-quality version.

New icon
- The paint well now stands on its own, without the cream tile behind it, so it sits neatly
  on any desktop, light or dark.
